2. Standard support scope

2.1Standard Support Services apply to each Customer Tenant for which all applicable Fees are being paid to Opsi.

2.2Standard Support Services are provided during Business Hours only.

2.3Standard Support Services include, at no additional cost:

  • Updates and required Patches and Fixes, applied by Opsi to the Hosted Service, as necessary to maintain the correct functionality of ORBIT;
  • remote support from the Opsi support team, including a ticketing system that keeps a record of communications and actions relating to each Ticket for review and reporting; and
  • the targeted response and fix times in paragraph 8 of this Schedule.

3. Opsi responsibilities

3.1Opsi will provide the Standard Support Services to Customer for every Tenant for which the relevant Fees are being paid.

3.2Opsi will:

  • be responsible for the management and supervision of the Standard Support Services; and
  • give Customer reasonable advance notice of Updates that materially change how Authorised Users work with ORBIT, including the scope of the change and its timing. Emergency Updates and security fixes may be applied without advance notice and will be communicated as soon as practicable.

4. Customer responsibilities

4.1Before an issue is escalated to Opsi, it must be reviewed and troubleshot by a designated super user or administrator who has been trained on ORBIT and knows Customer’s business processes and configuration well enough to make a reasonable assessment of the problem and to describe it clearly.

5. Support process

5.1The following procedure applies for logging a Ticket:

  • the Authorised User reports the issue to the designated super user or administrator;
  • if the super user cannot resolve it, the super user logs a Ticket with Opsi and, in urgent cases, follows up immediately with a call to Opsi’s helpdesk;
  • until the issue is Resolved, Opsi provides progress updates to the super user at reasonable intervals;
  • Opsi notifies Customer when a Ticket is closed, signifying that the problem has been Resolved; and
  • for Critical incidents, Opsi provides a root cause analysis on request within 5 business days of the incident being closed.

6. Updates

6.1Updates are released to the production Hosted Service only after appropriate testing.

7. Backups

7.1Opsi maintains backups of production data on the Hosted Service at least once a day.

7.2Opsi maintains a formal disaster recovery plan. Details, including the recovery time objective, are available to Customer on request.

8. Severity levels and response times

8.1The following severity levels apply:

Critical
an incident causing severe disruption to Customer’s business, or which will do so if not dealt with immediately, with Authorised Users having no readily available alternative way of performing their normal work.
High
an incident where a major component is degraded or causing significant business impact and Authorised Users are having difficulty performing part of their normal work.
Medium
an incident where a component is not functioning but is not critical, with limited business impact, or a service request.
Low
a request for change or enhancement, or a minor incident with little or no business impact.

8.2The targeted response and fix times are:

8.3The targeted maximum downtime resulting from Severity 1 or Severity 2 issues is less than 0.5%, measured over a calendar month.

9. Escalation

9.1If Customer is not satisfied with Opsi’s progress on a Critical or High severity issue, the issue will be escalated to more senior management within Opsi.

10. Excluded services

10.1The following are excluded from Standard Support Services but may be provided by Opsi as Additional Support Services at additional cost:

  1. training beyond any onboarding included in an Order Form;
  2. customisations, enhancements and extensions not explicitly included in the licensed scope;
  3. support for issues that do not result from an ORBIT fault, that are first-level support, or that result from a lack of Authorised User training;
  4. Customer-side testing, change management or go-live support for Updates;
  5. resolving issues caused by changes made to Customer Data, configuration or integrations without the involvement of Opsi personnel;
  6. services relating to third-party software, ERP systems, equipment or services not procured from Opsi;
  7. repetitive questions or errors resulting from incomplete training;
  8. errors for which Opsi has provided correction procedures that Customer has not implemented within a reasonable time;
  9. errors resulting from misuse, or use not in accordance with the Documentation;
  10. configuration, maintenance, troubleshooting and similar work on Customer devices, including Field Users’ phones and tablets;
  11. user errors, such as problems caused by incorrect setup, incorrect master data, user actions that conflict with the Documentation or Customer’s own procedures, or failure to perform required administrative duties; and
  12. basic operational or training-related enquiries.

11. Remote support

11.1All Standard Support Services are provided remotely.

11.2In-person support is provided only under a separate agreement for Additional Support Services.

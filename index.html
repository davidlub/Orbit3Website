How It Works

From raw data to deployed territories in three steps

No GIS expertise required. Upload a spreadsheet, set your parameters, and ORBIT does the hard work.

1

Load your master data

Import stores and reps from Excel into your organisation's master data, grouped by region. GPS, call frequencies, contract dates, pre-assigned groups and shopping centres are all respected — and never split.

2

Cut territories & build routes

Pick a region and run the algorithm — balanced, contiguous territories in under a minute. Then schedule visits into Repeating calendars or Dynamic urgency-driven days.

3

Dispatch & keep it live

Push plans to reps' phones and track every visit as it happens. When your data changes, flow the updates straight into live plans — no full replan.

Territory Cutting

Perfectly balanced regions. Every time.

ORBIT's algorithm combines weighted K-means clustering with geographic contiguity constraints and iterative boundary tuning. The result: territories that are compact, connected, and fair — without hours of manual adjustment.

  • ±7% weight balance guaranteed across all territories
  • Respects pre-assigned stores and shopping centre groupings
  • Guarantees geographic connectivity — no islands, ever
  • Compare 2 to 30 territory counts in a single algorithm run
  • Manual refinement on a live map with instant visual feedback
Tactical Planning

Every rep, every store, every day.

Once territories are cut, ORBIT solves the scheduling problem: which stores does each rep visit on which day, in what order, to minimize drive time while fitting within working hours.

  • Day-by-day route optimization across your full planning period
  • Configurable working hours per day — even different hours each day
  • Start from territory centre or a single shared depot
  • Multi-week planning periods from 1 to 8 weeks
  • Compare coverage and distance across all territories side by side
  • Export daily plans to Excel for immediate dispatch
Daily Planning

Two ways to plan every day.

Take a plan live and ORBIT drives it onto a real calendar — with the mode that fits how you work.

  • Repeating — a fixed weekly cycle mapped across the planning period, so reps follow a predictable, recurring beat.
  • Dynamic — each day is built fresh from store urgency: days since last visit versus the target cadence, seeded by each store's last-visit date.
  • Contract start/end dates gate visits automatically — nothing is scheduled outside a store's active window.
  • Working days, leave, and per-rep hours are all respected.
  • A live dashboard tracks completed vs due, tempo, and off-location visits.
Rep Mobile App

Your reps' route, in their pocket.

No app store, no install headaches — just a link. The rep app is a fast, installable web app that works even when there's no signal.

  • See today's stops in order, with contact, address and tap-to-call phone
  • One tap to Start and Finish each visit — dwell time captured automatically
  • GPS recorded on every button press, flagging off-location visits
  • Fully offline: work all day with no coverage, syncs the moment you reconnect
  • Turn-by-turn hand-off to the phone's maps app for navigation
Field Sales · SFA

Turn every planned visit into an order.

Switch on Field Sales and the route the rep is already following becomes an order pad — catalogue, tiered pricing, VAT, signatures and a live sales view, all built into the same ORBIT they use to plan.

Order Capture

An order pad in every rep's pocket.

At each stop the rep opens the store's order screen, adds lines from your catalogue at that store's own price tier, and captures the owner's signature to confirm — online or offline.

  • Prices resolve automatically from the store's customer tier
  • VAT applied per line and per Division, snapshotted at capture
  • On-screen customer signature confirms the order
  • Fully offline — orders sync the moment signal returns
  • Every order is filterable, searchable and exportable to Excel

Flow-Through Updates

Your data changes. Your plans keep up.

Stores open, close and move; contracts and call frequencies shift. ORBIT treats master data as the source of truth and flows changes straight into your live plans — so a small change never means a full replan.

  • See exactly what changed — added, removed and edited stores, each tagged with its impact
  • New stores are auto-assigned to the right territory and slotted into the nearest route day
  • Removed stores drop out of routes and future visits automatically
  • Dynamic plans absorb changes on the next generated day; Repeating routes are re-optimised in place
  • Completed and in-progress visits are always preserved
  • Per-store day restrictions are respected — a Monday-only customer stays on a Monday
